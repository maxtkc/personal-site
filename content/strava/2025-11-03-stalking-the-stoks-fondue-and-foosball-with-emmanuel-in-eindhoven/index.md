Just one year since me and Melissa Stok 's brother last had fondue. This time, the roof deck was open! He seems to be doing quite well 🫡

Ran to Belgium! Fueled by 4 stroop waffles and a bottle of water, wearing only the highest quality trail runners (the bottom of Allbirds is incredibly flat). I was hoping to find a nice Belgian waffle right at the border but no such luck. Just some sort of castle thing with a weird sculpture out front.

Included pics of the train before, game night with Donny, and whatever other pics I took. Apparently Strava has a very high limit.
