Apparently I missed some great aurora days. Whoops. Good thing I've got three months with the rail pass. I made it back. Happened to see two of my train buddies from the way out (one getting on the train as I got off, the other staying at the same hostel).

I took a meeting on my way down from this little hike, and my phone battery dropped suddenly and died. Unfortunately I had forgotten to start my watch until a little ways in, so I had to do a little navigating by memory to get back to the hostel.

Kiruna is a very interesting town. Mining x Tourism
