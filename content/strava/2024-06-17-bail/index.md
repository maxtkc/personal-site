Missed my flight. I think it's a sign. I'm gonna go sailing

I went at 10 to mail my pocket knives home to the post office. At 10:40 I talked to someone. My bus was 11 right outside. At 11:30 I finally got done mailing my package. The 12pm bus with 2 transfers got me to the airport at 1:10. I had a 1:40 flight but it's a tiny airport. I forgot to check in online. You're not allowed to check in within 1h of your flight. I went through a little crisis, watched an episode of suits, called my mom, and realized that it was time to sail. Contacted pancakes and found a 9 hour ferry leaving at 4:25, a 1h40m walk from the airport. By the time I decided to go for it, it was past 3, so I ran walked with front and back bags scaring all the cows with the flippity flopping of my flip flops. Made it with ten minutes to spare!
