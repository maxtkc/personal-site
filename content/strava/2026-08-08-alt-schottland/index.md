After facing major delays on the British east coast mainline, I made it to Edinburgh with my Brompton and dropped off my suit on my way to Dundee. We've been enjoying the right to roam up here and people seem quite friendly about it. I'm currently in the tent, listening to the waves crash on the shore. My experiment with the Brompton has gone very well and I'm excited for more tomorrow.
