I happened to move to the one city in Germany with an alumni from my frat... And he's a pretty cool guy! With him, his gf, his gf, his gfs friend, and a guy I emailed on the internet because he does some cool transit who now works with me at my company, we hiked to St Peter's for a very nice meal.

Regarding units of measurement, I am onboard with metric for length and weight. However, Fahrenheit has some advantages over Celsius for air temp. 0 is cold, 100 is hot, 50 is eh. Also, I don't like the "," in European math. Let's swap that back. "," is supposed to break up parts of sentences or numbers. "." Is a larger and more important break. Also, can Europeans not make csv's with decimals? That's kinda rough. Maybe we need a compromise.
