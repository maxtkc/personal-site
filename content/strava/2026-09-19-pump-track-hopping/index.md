Did a couple pump tracks on the way north, as well as a bit of cobble, and some steep bits where I bailed to the grass on the side. Was accompanied by Fabio for the way north, then did a little loop back to the S-Bahn. It's crazy how beautiful the view from the S-Bahn is here.

Yesterday I came home to a big gathering outside my house. They had a slideshow projected across the stream with a speaker placed in a stand in the water narrating. Following this, some musicians and singers entered the stream to perform. All directly in front of my door. Very cool

I made my first LinkedIn post! If you like trains, check it out
