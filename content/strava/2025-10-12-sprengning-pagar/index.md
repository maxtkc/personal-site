I'm at the top of the ski slope in Narvik, having seen zero people on the way up because I guess everyone's gone to bed, and trying to decrypt this sign. "Explosion in progress"

Honestly that would have been cool to see. I didn't see the northern lights, but it was pretty cloudy. Hopefully before I go south I'll get a sighting. 

I'm having a lot of fun with this camera...

So I'm posting photos here from the train trip up, which was 5h late, providing ample time to make friends up and down the train.
