Had to make sure I hit all of the things any good route has:
- trains and tracks
- a coastal factory 
- an industrial themed playground
- fun gravel so you're underbiking
- swedish grandpa's at an rc airfield with ice cream
- tallbikes by the beach
- art
- animals making faces at the camera
- piers so you can photograph your bike in all it's glory
- trailside snacks and friends to pick them with
