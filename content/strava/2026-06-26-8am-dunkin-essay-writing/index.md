When you get up at 6 you see some interesting stuff. Early risers are scary. Saw a guy locked in writing an essay at the Porter dunkin. What an odd spot. Didn't see anyone eating any worms though, but I wouldn't be surprised if Dunkin let a few slip in.

Took a deep dive into hardcore running culture with tracksmith. Matthew Radzihovsky kept me safe from the other runners and turned the 5k "fun" run into something worth waking up before the crack of dawn for. Apparently you're only cool if you do a sub 2:30 marathon. I think I ate the most bagels though so who's really winning.

Tossed in some pictures from some cool things around Boston and the weekend. Tall ships are different than I expected.
