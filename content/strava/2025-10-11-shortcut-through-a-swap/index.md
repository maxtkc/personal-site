I'm sitting enjoying this amazing pizza and unlimited salad and buns with wet feet waiting for my train. After wandering out of Uppsala (what a fun name), I quickly found some trails to enjoy the foliage from. Trying to maximize trails and seeing the bike route take your big detour, I decided to venture on some longer trails in the woods. I got made fun of by some Swedes and did a lot of bike pushing. They also have these fake blueberries that were disappointing (I looked it up, I forget what they're called).

My camera setup was very fun so I took tons of pictures. Boy do I look goofy. Ah well. I'm very excited to be back on the train tonight. I gotta do a bit more planning.
