Discussed with Maaya Prasad  how guys (myself unfortunately very much included) are often quite bad at active listening and forget lots of things and refuse to write things down to remember them... So here's a summary of our conversation. She first informed me that she was zwifting, and suspected that I might judge her for that (which I did). Then she suggested that I learn how to dive so we can dive together. She responded to my claims of weak ears with lots of workarounds and great points involving science and pressure and things. We then had a brief discussion about a photo I had taken and the composition about it, and closed it off with a rebuke of the male species as poor listeners (and we have come full circle).

I saw Jackson Moore-Otto off on his way through the noreaster back to Boston, went to a rooftop concert, walked for miles and miles around Berlin, filmed some content for Instagram (which I need to edit), met lots of people, went shopping for baggy pants (it's a thing I guess), went to the Hausberg of Berlin (its not completely flat) and saw the sunset, hit some solid tgtgs... Not a bad weekend!
