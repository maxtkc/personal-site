This is how the European party. All. Night. Long. Or at least my version of it. Pretty crazy to find myself hiking in shorts and a t shirt up the local ski slope right after the groomer drives into the garage. 

The trail is the Westweg. It goes further south and further north. Lots more to explore.

I went from s train to s train. I had just enough time for a quick grocery shop before my train home, but the ppl in front of me failed to be efficient and this lead to me spending an additional hour in Schopfheim. They're having elections tomorrow! A guy told me all about the pre ww history in Germany that occurred in this little village. Very interesting if true.

Listened to Educated from head to tail, as well as half of careless people. It's interesting reading about the US from the outside.
