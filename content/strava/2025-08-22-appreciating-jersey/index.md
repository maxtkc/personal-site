Very excited to have Jonathan Adler be officially part of the family!

I was shitting on Jersey on the way over, taking pictures from the freeway. In Jonathan's toast he mentioned wanting his family to see the nice parts of Jersey. I don't think I expected the venue, so close to where I come every year for Thanksgiving, to be so nice. The route back took me by some farms, on parts of the Lawrence Hopewell trail, and past my grandmas memorial stone. I spooked a few wild animals and a horse!

I still don't know how I feel about Jersey as a whole, but in every place there's nice and there's not so nice and that's ok.
