There's something pretty about trains and trucks and big industrial buildings in the morning. I had to jump a few barriers to do the route back from the schrafts candy factory (where I visited my favorite candy factory employee, who did give me candy) but it's a pleasant route between a newly built business district, a recycling plant, the railroad, the highway, and Boston sand and gravel.
