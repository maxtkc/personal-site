We're sitting at taco bell with tinder and got 40 likes real quick. Unfortunately, no one was reading our bio about wanting a place to crash so they were all pretty far away.

But then, lo and behold, a 2 hour break in the rain. A perfect chance to book it the 13 miles to the camp spot with the nice gazebo!

But then, Julius gets a pinch flat. Fortunately, we've got a half working pump and two CO2 cartridges.

But then, I accidentally blew through the first cartridge because the valve was open. 

Ok the rest was all good. The second one worked, about to go to sleep now.
