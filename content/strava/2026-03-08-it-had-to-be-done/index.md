On the way out a guy on an MTB points at me and says to his son, Frelo MTB!

I thought I'd get some comments from the rule following Germans, but everyone was very supportive of my 2 euro rental. One guy told me to skip the top bit because it was too steep. I do not regret following his advice.

I will soon be purchasing a bike with suspension as to not break my wrists and get shook to bits.
