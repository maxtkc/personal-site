Maybe that was lost in translation but it did taste like butt.

Also had a big block of fried cheese. Was having a bit of trouble keeping all down on the windy bus ride up the mountain.

Love bus 66. Pretty sweet to have a city bus with a big MTB rack, ski storage,  and decent frequency going up to the mountain. Thanks for the tip, Rumen Hristov !

Low visibility. Was barely dressed properly. Wouldn't want to do it any slower. Passed a bunch of Backcountry skiers skinning up and now I'm questioning the sport. Kinda slow 🤔

Visit the US embassy this morning. Pretty cool. Strange to see a USPS office here
