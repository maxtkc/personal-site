Saw this a few months ago and have wanted to try since. Pretty fun! However I found the protection only to go so far.
Fabio came along and tried it out too. I do want to come back and get a bit better. 

The weather and view was amazing. A little rain cooled it down.

It's a fun crowd. Also, it'll be nice to have some confidence and control when I'm longboarding down something in a city.
