I met Daren with my dad before starting college on the Great Divide Mountain Bike Route (GDMBR) in Del Norte, NM. Now he lives in Scotland, so we got together today for a day ride! On foldies! It's pretty cool to meet people on the trail and get to know them off the trail. It's a small world

We found tons and tons of wild blueberries. They were everywhere! It was paradise. Also we found a few raspberries.

Why doesn't Strava have a foldy option? It's a whole different experience...
