The French are an odd bunch, for sure. But maybe everyone's just jealous about the food.

Fixed my kit lens today! I have been using my new lens for a while, so it was not urgent, but it's nice to have options again. See that little black speck in front of the ring? That little thing jammed it so my camera would bug out with it. Costs about a dollar to replace, but about an hour of fiddling.

This sunset spot is just a few minutes walk from my flat. Come visit!
