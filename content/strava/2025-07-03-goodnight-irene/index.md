Got to see Dave Connelly at a little Brooklyn jam. He seems to be socializing well with the older folks here. We were told after the jam that he had an excellent baratone. Apparently not many people have that. But something about him being very musical allows for this. Good shit.

No pictures of it but had some fantastic street tacos with Seth Massey. He has an optimistic view of Q timing though. Maybe I'm not doing it right, but it seems to always stop somewhere for a while and wait.

Also, NYC parks are pretty damn nice. Prospect Park is fantastic and I think I could spend many days doing all of the different sports available at the Brooklyn bridge park.
