When I realized how far the summit was from the city, I decided I'd hike over to the bus to get a little closer. However, even the bus stop was far away. When I got there, I was just a minute early so I didn't know if the bus had passed already. After five or ten minutes, I got restless and decided it would be stupid to wait if I had maybe missed it, so I left the stop. Of course, the bus comes a minute later. I tried to run back and catch it to no avail.

Dispirited, I walked into a nice store with many old ladies milling about. That's when I found it.

With the first gulp, I was reinvigorated. Then I had a field day with my new model. I didn't make it hard for the French to judge me, but if they're gonna judge me anyways, might as well make it fun for them.

Julia Wyatt  Hot dog season is over, it's la saison du tomate

I found a fountain partway up, but it wasn't labeled for drinking or not, so I asked an old guy in French and successfully made it though the brief conversation (I think)!

On the way back, I met an American kid at the bus stop who studied astrophysics. He gave me the run down on how we end up colonizing mars. It made a little too much sense, but I'm still skeptical.

Anybody recognize those bikes? Reminds me of home. It's honestly really nice. I miss blue bikes (it's still the background shot on my phone 🤣 (I should probably change that)).

Also, is that a robotic golf cart? How does he control where it goes? Does it automatically follow the ball?
