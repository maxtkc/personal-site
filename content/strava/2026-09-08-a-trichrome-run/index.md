First, I climbed up the hill by ETH, taking pictures as I went. Next, I joined 5 Swiss men for the Tuesday night run club with the earliest start. Finally, I ran to the Indian store to grab paneer for dinner.

I showed up for the dinner party real sweaty, showered, was cooked for by my gracious host and photography teacher and new friend, then walked back to Zürich Hbf for my train back home.

The train back is the OBB nightjet from Zürich to Amsterdam. The Dutch are on strike so it's stopping in Köln. DB is having its usual problems, so I'm writing this at Basel Bad Bf, the sad little station on the other side of the river from Basel SBB where we are sitting and racking up delays. At SBB I at least had WiFi...
