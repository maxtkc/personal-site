On my way to Split... Caught the first train out of Freiburg in that direction and ran into Sophia D !

Took my morning meeting in a park then walked around until I got back to the station where my train was waiting for me. Just kidding! It's late. The door for the bikes is broken so people have to wheel them down the isle to get on and off.

Apparently they're not just trying to do math, it's some tradition and those aren't pluses, they're crosses. Maybe, alternatively, religion was just a misunderstanding of math?
