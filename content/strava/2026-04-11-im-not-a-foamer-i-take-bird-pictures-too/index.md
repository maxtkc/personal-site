After a wonderful tour of Den Haag and language lesson from Seamus Joyce-Johnson, I headed back to pick up my too good to go and almost forgotten pizza I had ordered the night before, "just in case".

This is the last weekend on my rail pass. I've enjoyed it tremendously, however I am very excited to spend next Saturday back in Freiburg. I'll finally go to parkrun, then to the trolleycar club, then get some leftover kombucha, head back to my apartment and play some Catan and read some of my German cartoons and Kinderbücher (whine the poo!). I have so much to explore there.

Maybe someday Boston will adjust their pass to be more accessible. It encourages riders to ride more and it improves the experience. It saves money for those who ride the most. And it saves time while boarding. Software companies have figured it out, transit is taking a bit longer.
